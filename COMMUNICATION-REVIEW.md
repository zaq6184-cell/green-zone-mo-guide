# Review: Communication on shift (communication skills in the Green Zone)

**Drafted 10 Nov 2026** for the entry *Communication on shift* (`#communication`, `src/parts/data10.js`). Not built, not tested in the app, not published: the integrator adds `data10.js` to `build.js` and `check-data.js` and draws the eight figures listed at the end.

- **The request:** Krishna asked to "create, research online in the topic communication skills in green zone and make it interactive premium, and add it into the app", and gave an ED quick card on aggressive patients (`sources/communication/Aggressive_Patient_Communication_ED_Quick_Card.pdf`, text in `quick-card.txt`).
- **What the entry is:** a rule entry (`grp:'rules'`, `sub:'protocol'`, `isnew:true`) that starts from the situation in front of the MO and walks it step by step: an angry or aggressive patient or relative (safety first), a complaint about the wait, bad news, explaining a discharge, handing over or referring by phone, and no shared language. 41 nodes (10 questions, 15 steps, 16 outcomes), 6 notes tabs (Phrases, De-escalation, Bad news, Discharge talk, Handover, Sources) plus References (17 references), 8 figure keys, and see-also links both ways with four related entries.
- **Rule 8:** every line carries its source in brackets, using the short names explained in the Sources tab; every outcome has a `gap` line naming its sources. No dose, drug or clinical criterion is given: BARS, sedation and restraint stay on the ebook's *Acute behavioural disturbance* pathway (p5–9), which the entry links to. Published sources are summarised in the guide's own words, with no quote longer than 15 words; the card's phrases are used as printed, because Krishna supplied it for the app.
- **Size:** `JSON.stringify(P.communication).length` is about 38,700 characters, the largest rule entry (Code Blue 27,131; animal bites 20,159) because it covers six situations. A first draft was 45,240; the cut removed notes that repeated the steps and shortened the references and source lines. If that is still too heavy, the aggression branch (about a third of it) could become its own entry.

## 6. Left out, and why

- **Drugs, doses, BARS and restraint**: on the ebook's pathway (rule 5); the entry links to it and to `#calc-calm`.
- **Waiting-time targets for MTS 4 and 5**: the 90 minutes for "green" cases comes from a 2019 parliamentary reply and another hospital's client charter, before MTS 2022; no department document in the app gives HKL's target, so the entry gives no number.
- **Physical breakaway and restraint techniques**: training, not a phone guide (NICE NG10 puts them in staff training).
- **The nine GRIEV_ING letters**: read only in a secondary summary.
- **A guideline-level source for telling a family of a death**: the death branch (`death`, `after`) leans on one source more than any other branch does: the CSTS fact sheet (US, Uniformed Services University; undated; no reuse statement), with Hobgood 2005 and SPIKES behind some lines. One search for an RCEM or ACEM document found none; the European Society for Emergency Medicine's *End of life care in emergency departments* recommendations (Sep 2017), which have sections on breaking bad news and on bereaved relatives, came up, but their PDF link (eusem.org) answers 404. If Krishna prefers a guideline-level or Malaysian source, the branch should be re-sourced from it.
- **SPIKES's own example sentences**: copyright; the entry's sentences are the guide's own.
- **Project BETA's staffing of a show of force (a team of 4 to 6)**: its context was not clear from the text read; "call for backup" from the card covers it.
- **Service-recovery models (HEARD, AIDET and others) and I-PASS**: no primary source was read; ISBAR was chosen for handover, as the caller suggested (a 2026 Malaysian study, seen only as a search snippet, built an ED handover tool on ISBAR domains).
- **The Star's account of how MOH inquiries run**: background only; the HKL page is the department's own source.
- **Malaysian law on assaulting health staff**: not researched; the MOH 2023 guideline on violence against staff would cover notification.

Suggested additions to `CONFIRM` in `engine.js` (the colour codes card and the interpreter memo already have items):
