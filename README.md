# Green Zone MO Guide, version 8

A guide for medical officers working in the Green Zone (MTS levels 4–5), Emergency & Trauma Department, Hospital Kuala Lumpur. It is built for use during a shift, on a phone first. It comes from *The Clinical Pathway in Emergency for Medical Officer* (HKL ED, 2025), which stays the approved source, with department documents from the MO Drive and the triage course folder added as labelled notes. It is a reference tool only and holds no patient data.

## What the app does

**During a shift**
- **Home:** a bento of tiles, each showing real data from the guide: the zone map (five stops of a Green Zone visit; tap one for what happens there and the rules that apply), Code Blue and Code Stroke with Copy, quick routes with how each pathway can end, your saved and recent pathways, the number of pathways and rules, the discharge check, the calculators, three more numbers, a pearl for the shift, the three rules to keep and what is new.
- **Search from anywhere:** press **/** or Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac), tap a search box, or use the Search tab on a phone. It finds pathways, rules, steps, numbers and department notes as you type.
- **Moving tiles:** each topic tile, and Code Blue and Code Stroke, shows a glass sculpture of its body system made in Flow Studio, moving in a quiet 8-second loop (the heart beats, the lungs breathe). The other home tiles move too: a book, a shield, a doorway, a gauge, a compass and a pearl. So do the Pathways section bars and the Shift tools headers. A loop plays only while its tile is on screen, and a phone plays at most six at once; Settings, Moving tiles turns them off.
- **Pathways:** 23 ebook pathways in six body-system groups, and 15 rules and protocols (8 on the Green Zone flow and referrals, 7 department protocols). Filter as you type, or tap a group. Star a pathway to keep it on the home screen.
- **The step screen:** one question per card, with a progress bar and **Your route** beside it (on a phone, the answers so far as chips you can tap to go back). **Can still end in** shows which outcome boxes are still possible from where you are. Checklists highlight the answer that fits what you ticked. It ends in the outcome card, in the ebook's colours: red for admit, yellow for observation, green for discharge, violet for referral. **Copy outcome** and **Copy advice** put the text on the clipboard. Below it, tabs for discharge advice, practical concerns, department notes, tables and references. On a keyboard, 1 to 9 answer and Backspace steps back.
- **Whole pathway:** every step of a pathway on one sheet, numbered, with each answer pointing to the step it leads to. Your route is lit, and the outcomes you can no longer reach are dimmed. Tap any step to go straight to it.
- **Discharge safety check:** six things to tick before a patient goes home, shown on a route meter, and seven stop signs (for example a return within 48 hours, abdominal pain above 0, or a CT brain) that each tell you what to do instead.
- **Calculators:** eighteen, built only from the ebook and the department's notes, each naming its source, in eight groups with a glass sculpture each. Heart: the chest pain Obsess protocol check, the syncope risk check, SVT with esmolol by weight, and hypertensive urgency with the ED drug table. Poisoning and allergy: paracetamol overdose by the time since ingestion, and the anaphylaxis drugs with fluids by weight. Thirteen emergency scores our sources do not define or cover open on MDCalc, an outside site: TIMI, ABCD2, creatinine clearance, GCS, Wells for PE, PERC, QTc, Alvarado, Centor, Canadian C-spine, NEXUS, and the Ottawa ankle and knee rules. Dengue: shock index, pulse pressure and heart rate against the discharge criteria, and adjusted body weight. Doses: paracetamol and ketamine by weight, fondaparinux and Clexane for a confirmed DVT, and the total daily insulin dose. Scores: CURB-65 and Wells, with the pathway's next step. Pain and calm: FLACC, PAINAD and CPOT read on the MOH ladder, and BARS and RASS with weight-based lines from the medication table at BARS 7. Injuries: the head injury CT and safe discharge criteria. Kids: child doses and the SOAP dehydration check with ORS. Where a source stops, the calculator says so. Five children's tools our sources do not cover open on PEMCalc, an outside site. The weight carries across the dose calculators while the guide is open; nothing you type is saved.
- **Read aloud and watch:** the patient advice can be played aloud for a patient: both national CPG leaflets (dengue, Appendix 5B; cancer pain) in English and Bahasa Melayu, and the discharge advice of mild head injury, dengue and the Asthma Zone in English. Tap Listen; chips start a section, the section being read is outlined and the line being read is marked as the voice reaches it. The dengue leaflet also has a two-minute film in each language, every line on screen as it is read, with a quiet music bed. Nothing downloads until you tap.
- **Rabies and animal bites:** the HKL protocol (Oct 2022) step by step, from the risk checklist and the wound category to the vaccine course and RIG, with how to write each prescription and a writer that dates every dose from Day 0.
- **Numbers to know:** 14 extensions and lines in four groups, each with a copy button and a link to the rule it belongs to.
- **Links you can send:** every pathway and every step has its own address, for example `#renal-colic`, `#gz-rules--ivab` or `#calc-kids`.
- **Day and night:** a switch at the top of the home screen turns the night colours on and off. In night colours the tour plays a night version of the film and the poster page shows night versions of the sheets.
- **Settings:** light, dark or the device's theme (the same setting as the Day and Night switch; Auto follows the device again); standard or large text; moving tiles on or off; journey clips on or off; clear your saved list and history.
- **On a phone:** a floating tab bar (Home, Pathways, Search, Tools, Numbers). No video plays on the home screen, to save mobile data.
- **Install the guide** (Shift tools, the menu, Settings, or `#install`): the steps for the phone, tablet or computer in your hand, found from its browser and drawn step by step, with an Install button where the browser offers one, what is saved on the device and whether you are online. Choose another device to see its steps, or scan the code on a computer to open the page on your phone. In the claude.ai copy it links to the web app, which is the one that installs.
- **Install it on a phone, and use it without a signal** (the public web app only): open https://zaq6184-cell.github.io/green-zone-mo-guide/ once with a signal and it saves itself on the phone: the guide and its pictures (about 1.6 MB to download, much of which the page loads anyway). After that it opens on patchy ward Wi-Fi or with no signal at all. Films and sounds are not downloaded ahead: each is kept once you have played it through with a signal (from that same download, so nothing is downloaded twice), and then plays offline too. Settings, Offline and install, says what is saved.
  - **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install** when Chrome offers it, or open Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). The Green Zone icon opens it full screen; press and hold the icon for Search, Calculators and Numbers.
  - **Samsung Internet, Edge or Firefox on Android:** ≡ then **Add page to**, **Home screen** (Samsung); ••• then **Add to phone** (Edge); ⋮ then **Install** (Firefox).
  - **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. From Chrome or Edge it works the same way on iOS 16.4 or later.
  - **A computer (Chrome or Edge, on Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS):** the install icon at the right of the address bar. **Safari on a Mac** (Sonoma or later): **File**, then **Add to Dock**. **Firefox** on a computer cannot install apps: bookmark it, and the bookmark opens offline too.
  - When a new version is published, the phone picks it up the next time the guide opens with a signal; a page left open from an older version shows **New version ready** with Reload.

**For learning and new staff**
- **First-shift intro:** four quick screens with drawn, animated pictures: search, one question at a time, the one-way zone and the tools. The home screen offers it until you have seen it.
- **The patient journey:** five stops, each with an 8-second clip, a Listen button for the narration, and links to the rules at that stop. Play all runs through them in order.
- **The tour:** the 89-second explainer (rebuilt for version 8 in the new look, with the version 7 rules), in English or Bahasa Melayu (1:35) with the Narration switch under the video, in day or night colours to match the guide. Each language has 9 chapters, a clickable transcript, speeds from 0.75 to 1.5, and resume where you stopped.
- **Quiz:** 13 questions on the Green Zone rules and the department material, each with the reason and a link to the rule.
- **What's new:** the triage course folder and MO Drive run-throughs, and what is still to confirm.
- **Wall poster** and **Sources and about**.
