# Malay advice and journey lines: draft for review

Drafted 24 Nov 2026 for Krishna's check before anything is published (his "all five"). The Malay is in `src/parts/data31.js` (`ADVICE_MS`, `JOURNEY_MS`). The English stays the source (rules 5 and 8).

How it was drafted:
- One Malay line for each English line, in the same order.
- Every drug name, dose, number and time is kept as printed. No advice is added or left out.
- The register and terms follow the guide's own Malay leaflets (dengue CPG, cancer pain CPG) and the handbook's Malay discharge script, for example "ubat tahan sakit", "Dapatkan rawatan", "berurut, bekam atau gua sha", "temujanji susulan" and "rujuk doktor".
- The department's own terms are kept: EMACC, EMWATCH, CCA, slip hijau, triaj, discaj.

A note marks every line where a word, the register or the meaning needs a Malay-speaking doctor's eye. Lines without a note were judged straightforward, but please read them all.

## Mild head injury (`mild-tbi`), ebook

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | After a concussion, the patient should be accompanied by a responsible adult if possible | Selepas gegaran otak, pesakit sebaiknya ditemani oleh seorang dewasa yang bertanggungjawab, jika boleh | "Gegaran otak" for concussion is the usual MOH term. "Sebaiknya" ("should, ideally") is softer than "mesti"; this fits "if possible". |
| 1 | Go to the nearest Emergency Department for: loss of consciousness, disorientation, new deafness, imbalance, blurred or double vision, weakness of any limb, excessive sleepiness or drowsiness, vomiting, bleeding from either ear, fitting, or severe headache not relieved by painkillers | Pergi ke Jabatan Kecemasan yang terdekat jika berlaku: hilang kesedaran, keliru, pekak yang baru berlaku, hilang imbangan badan, penglihatan kabur atau berganda, lemah pada mana-mana tangan atau kaki, terlalu mengantuk atau sentiasa mahu tidur, muntah, berdarah dari mana-mana telinga, sawan, atau sakit kepala yang teruk dan tidak hilang dengan ubat tahan sakit | Check these. "Disorientation" is given as "keliru" (confused); "bingung" is another option. "Weakness of any limb" is given as "tangan atau kaki". "Excessive sleepiness or drowsiness" is given as "terlalu mengantuk atau sentiasa mahu tidur", to keep both words. "Fitting" is given as "sawan". "Pengsan" may be more everyday than "hilang kesedaran". |
| 2 | Do stay within reach of a telephone, rest, sleep regularly, and take painkillers such as paracetamol for headache | Pastikan telefon sentiasa dekat dengan anda, berehat, tidur dengan teratur, dan ambil ubat tahan sakit seperti paracetamol untuk sakit kepala | "Do" is carried by "Pastikan". "Sleep regularly" is given as "tidur dengan teratur". |
| 3 | Don't stay at home alone for 48 hours after leaving hospital | Jangan tinggal seorang diri di rumah selama 48 jam selepas keluar dari hospital | |
| 4 | Don't drink alcohol or take recreational drugs | Jangan minum arak atau mengambil dadah | "Recreational drugs" is given as "dadah" (illicit drugs), the everyday word. Check that this is the sense wanted. "Arak" for alcohol is also used in renal colic line 5, while gastroenteritis line 2 says "beralkohol". "Minuman beralkohol" or "minuman keras" are softer options. |
| 5 | Don't take aspirin or sleeping tablets | Jangan ambil aspirin atau pil tidur | |
| 6 | Don't play or train for any contact sport for at least 3 weeks without consulting your doctor | Jangan bermain atau berlatih sukan yang melibatkan perlanggaran badan sekurang-kurangnya 3 minggu tanpa berbincang dengan doktor anda | "Contact sport" has no settled Malay term. It is given as "sukan yang melibatkan perlanggaran badan"; "sukan lasak" means extreme sport, so it was not used. No examples were added. The first take read back as "pelanggaran" (violation), a near-homophone; the kept take reads "perlanggaran". "Pertembungan badan" would avoid the clash. Also check whether the line needs "selama" before "sekurang-kurangnya 3 minggu". |
| 7 | Don't drive | Jangan memandu | |

## Dengue (`dengue`), ebook

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Do: adequate bed rest | Lakukan: rehat secukupnya | "Lakukan:" carries "Do:" on lines 0 to 5. "Bed rest" is given as plain "rehat", as the dengue leaflet does ("Dapatkan rehat yang mencukupi"). |
| 1 | Do: adequate fluid intake (more than 8 glasses or 2 litres for an average person) | Lakukan: minum air secukupnya (lebih daripada 8 gelas atau 2 liter bagi orang biasa) | "Average person" is given as "orang biasa" ("an ordinary adult"). Check that this is the sense wanted. |
| 2 | Do: take paracetamol (not more than 4 g a day) | Lakukan: ambil paracetamol (tidak melebihi 4 g sehari) | Same words as the dengue leaflet ("tidak melebihi 4g sehari"). |
| 3 | Do: tepid sponging | Lakukan: lap badan dengan kain yang dibasahkan dengan air suam | The term is explained rather than borrowed. Check that "air suam" (lukewarm) is the right sense for "tepid". |
| 4 | Do: use mosquito repellent or rest under a mosquito net, even in the daytime | Lakukan: guna ubat penghalau nyamuk atau berehat di dalam kelambu, walaupun pada waktu siang | |
| 5 | Do: look for and remove mosquito breeding places in and around the home | Lakukan: cari dan hapuskan tempat pembiakan nyamuk di dalam dan di sekitar rumah | |
| 6 | Don't take NSAIDs (painkillers); if already taking them, consult your doctor | Jangan ambil NSAIDs (ubat tahan sakit); jika anda sudah mengambilnya, rujuk doktor anda | Kept as printed: "NSAIDs (ubat tahan sakit)". The CPG leaflet names them in full ("ubat anti-radang bukan steroid"), but that would add words the ebook does not print. Read aloud as N. S. A. I. D. |
| 7 | Antibiotics are not required | Antibiotik tidak diperlukan | |
| 8 | Don't use massage, cupping or quasa | Jangan berurut, berbekam atau buat gua sha | The ebook prints "quasa". The Malay uses "gua sha", as the CPG's Malay leaflet spells it. Check. |

## Asthma Zone (`asthma-zone`), department flow

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Preventer (budesonide MDI) every day; reliever (salbutamol MDI) when needed | Ubat pencegah (MDI budesonide) setiap hari; ubat pelega (MDI salbutamol) apabila perlu | "Pencegah" and "pelega" are the usual MOH asthma leaflet words. Some leaflets say "inhaler pencegah" or "inhaler pelega". MDI is kept and read as M. D. I. |
| 1 | Getting worse (waking at night, worse with a cold): reliever every 4 hours, keep taking the preventer, and come to hospital if not better, bringing the MDI and spacer | Semakin teruk (terjaga pada waktu malam, bertambah teruk ketika selesema): ambil ubat pelega setiap 4 jam, teruskan ubat pencegah, dan datang ke hospital jika tidak bertambah baik, dengan membawa MDI dan spacer | "Spacer" is kept in English, as staff say it. "With a cold" is given as "ketika selesema". |
| 2 | Severe (wheeze that does not settle, breathless, hard to talk): come straight to hospital or call an ambulance, taking 4 puffs of the reliever every 4 minutes on the way | Teruk (berdehit yang tidak reda, sesak nafas, susah bercakap): terus datang ke hospital atau hubungi ambulans, dan ambil 4 sedutan ubat pelega setiap 4 minit dalam perjalanan | Check these. "Wheeze" is given as "berdehit"; "bunyi berdesit" is another option. "Puffs" is given as "sedutan"; "semburan" or "puf" are other options. Check which word patients are taught at the Asthma Zone. |
| 3 | Wash the spacer once a week in soapy water, rinse and let it air dry; do not wipe it with a cloth or tissue or brush the inside | Basuh spacer seminggu sekali dengan air sabun, bilas dan biarkan ia kering sendiri; jangan lap dengan kain atau tisu, dan jangan berus bahagian dalamnya | |
| 4 | Know your triggers: smoke, animal fur, cold air, dust, exercise and infections | Kenali pencetus asma anda: asap, bulu haiwan, udara sejuk, habuk, senaman dan jangkitan | "Asma" is added after "pencetus" for sense. Drop it if you want the English word for word. |

## Gastroenteritis (`gastroenteritis`), ebook

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Continue taking prescribed medications as directed | Teruskan mengambil ubat yang diberi oleh doktor seperti yang diarahkan | "Prescribed" is given as "yang diberi oleh doktor". |
| 1 | Drink plenty of fluid (ORS, clear soups, diluted fruit juices) to prevent dehydration | Minum banyak air (ORS, sup jernih, jus buah yang dicairkan) untuk mengelakkan kekurangan air dalam badan | "Dehydration" is given as "kekurangan air dalam badan". "Dehidrasi" is the formal word. ORS is kept and read as O. R. S. (the dengue leaflet writes "larutan garam rehidrasi (ORS)"). |
| 2 | Avoid caffeinated, alcoholic and sugary drinks, as they can worsen diarrhoea | Elakkan minuman berkafein, beralkohol dan bergula, kerana ia boleh memburukkan cirit-birit | |
| 3 | Get plenty of rest | Rehat secukupnya | |
| 4 | Hand hygiene, especially after using the toilet and before eating | Jaga kebersihan tangan, terutamanya selepas menggunakan tandas dan sebelum makan | "Basuh tangan" would be plainer, but narrower than "hand hygiene". |
| 5 | Seek medical help for persistent vomiting or inability to keep fluids down, blood in stools or persistent high fever, or severe abdominal pain or worsening symptoms | Dapatkan rawatan jika muntah berterusan atau setiap kali minum dimuntahkan semula, terdapat darah dalam najis atau demam tinggi yang berterusan, atau sakit perut yang teruk atau simptom semakin teruk | Check these. "Inability to keep fluids down" is given as "setiap kali minum dimuntahkan semula" (whatever is drunk comes back up). "Seek medical help" is given as "Dapatkan rawatan". |

## Renal colic (`renal-colic`), ebook

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Stay hydrated: drink 6 to 8 glasses of water a day as directed, to help decrease pain and flush blockages from the urinary tract | Minum air secukupnya: minum 6 hingga 8 gelas air sehari seperti yang diarahkan, untuk membantu mengurangkan sakit dan mengeluarkan sumbatan dari saluran kencing | "Flush blockages" is given as "mengeluarkan sumbatan" (bring out). |
| 1 | Strain urine every time you pass urine, and bring any stones passed to the doctor for investigation | Tapis air kencing setiap kali anda membuang air kecil, dan bawa batu yang keluar kepada doktor untuk diperiksa | |
| 2 | Take regular pain medications as directed by the doctor | Ambil ubat tahan sakit secara berkala seperti yang diarahkan oleh doktor | "Regular" is read as "on a schedule" ("secara berkala"). Check that this is the intended sense rather than "usual". |
| 3 | Seek help at the nearest hospital if pain, fever or vomiting worsens, or for changes in urine: less urine, cloudiness, foul smell | Dapatkan bantuan di hospital yang terdekat jika sakit, demam atau muntah bertambah teruk, atau jika air kencing berubah: kencing berkurang, keruh, berbau busuk | |
| 4 | Keep active; kidney stones are more common if you are not active or sit much of the day | Kekal aktif; batu karang lebih kerap berlaku jika anda kurang aktif atau banyak duduk sepanjang hari | "Kidney stones" is given as "batu karang", the everyday word ("batu ginjal" is the formal one). |
| 5 | Rest, and abstain from alcohol and seafood | Berehat, dan jauhi arak dan makanan laut | |

## Abdominal pain (`abdominal-pain`), ebook

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Return to hospital immediately if pain worsens or new symptoms appear, such as vomiting, diarrhoea, fever or bleeding from the back passage | Datang semula ke hospital dengan segera jika sakit bertambah teruk atau timbul simptom baru, seperti muntah, cirit-birit, demam atau berdarah dari dubur | "Back passage" is given as "dubur". The handbook's Malay script says "najis berdarah"; "dubur" is closer to the English. |
| 1 | Avoid caffeine, fatty foods and spicy foods | Elakkan kafein, makanan berlemak dan makanan pedas | |
| 2 | Take simple analgesia such as paracetamol and anti-reflux medications | Ambil ubat tahan sakit biasa seperti paracetamol, dan ubat anti-refluks | Check. "Anti-refluks" is a loan word; many patients would say "ubat gastrik". That gloss was not added, to keep to the ebook's words. |
| 3 | Return for follow-up at EMACC or EMWATCH in 24 to 48 hours | Datang semula untuk rawatan susulan di EMACC atau EMWATCH dalam masa 24 hingga 48 jam | Read as E. M. A. C. C. and E. M. Watch, as in the English clip. |

## Hypoglycaemia (`hypoglycaemia`), ebook

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Avoid driving, cycling, swimming or heavy machinery for the next 24–48 hours | Elakkan memandu, berbasikal, berenang atau mengendalikan jentera berat untuk 24–48 jam yang akan datang | |
| 1 | Hypoglycaemia awareness may be reduced for 48–72 hours: check blood sugar before meals, exercise and driving | Anda mungkin kurang dapat merasakan tanda gula darah rendah selama 48–72 jam: periksa gula darah sebelum makan, bersenam dan memandu | Check. "Hypoglycaemia awareness may be reduced" is put plainly as "anda mungkin kurang dapat merasakan tanda gula darah rendah" (you may not feel the signs of low sugar as well). |
| 2 | Always carry quick-acting carbohydrate (hard candy, dextrose tablets) | Sentiasa bawa karbohidrat yang cepat bertindak (gula-gula keras, tablet dekstrosa) | "Quick-acting" is given as "cepat bertindak". "Gula-gula keras" is hard candy. |
| 3 | Record all glucose readings and bring them to the next check-up | Catat semua bacaan gula darah dan bawa catatan itu semasa pemeriksaan yang akan datang | |
| 4 | Seek medical help if a hypo causes unconsciousness or seizures, does not improve with quick-acting carbohydrate, or blood sugar stays low | Dapatkan rawatan jika gula darah rendah (hipo) menyebabkan pengsan atau sawan, tidak pulih dengan karbohidrat yang cepat bertindak, atau gula darah kekal rendah | "Hipo" is kept in brackets, as patients hear it. |

## Patient journey stops (`JOURNEY` `say`), read to the MO

These are spoken to staff, not patients, so the register is professional. The local terms stay as staff use them.

| # | English | Malay | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Patients move one way. Triage, then secondary triage, then ECG, irrigation or dressing if needed, and on to you. | Pesakit bergerak sehala. Triaj, kemudian triaj sekunder, kemudian ECG, cucian luka atau balutan jika perlu, dan seterusnya kepada anda. | "Triaj sekunder" is the Malay spelling. The ED extension list prints the counter as "Triage Sekunder", so check which the department says. "Irrigation" is given as "cucian luka" (wound irrigation, as in the stop's own items). ECG is read as E. C. G. |
| 2 | You fill in the green slip. For the diagnosis, open its pathway. | Anda mengisi slip hijau. Untuk diagnosis, buka laluannya. | "Laluan" for pathway, as in the Malay intro. |
| 3 | The treatment room calls the queue number, carries out each order, and documents it. | Bilik rawatan memanggil nombor giliran, menjalankan setiap arahan, dan merekodkannya. | |
| 4 | Waiting for results? Send the patient to the CCA. The elderly, ACS, or anyone needing stat drugs never waits outside. | Menunggu keputusan? Hantar pesakit ke CCA. Warga emas, pesakit ACS, atau sesiapa yang memerlukan ubat segera, tidak sekali-kali menunggu di luar. | Check. "Stat drugs" is given as "ubat segera". "Tidak sekali-kali" (never) is strong; "tidak boleh menunggu di luar" is another option. |
| 5 | Before discharge, reassess and review every result. After a CT brain, ask the EP to review, and book EMACC or EMWATCH follow-up. Unsure, or the disposition is stuck? Call the EP in charge. | Sebelum discaj, nilai semula pesakit dan semak setiap keputusan. Selepas CT otak, minta EP menyemak, dan tempah susulan EMACC atau EMWATCH. Tidak pasti, atau keputusan disposisi tersekat? Hubungi EP yang bertugas. | Check these. "CT brain" is given as "CT otak"; staff may simply say "CT brain". "Disposition is stuck" is given as "keputusan disposisi tersekat". "EP in charge" is given as "EP yang bertugas"; "EP in-charge" is also used on the floor. |

## Recordings: words to listen for

The Malay clips (`app/audio/adv-<key>-ms.mp3`, `stop1-ms.mp3` to `stop5-ms.mp3`) were recorded on 24 Nov 2026 in VoiceStudio's "Malay Kore - Female - TTS Studio" voice. Each take was checked by machine transcription, not by ear. The transcriber respelled these words in the kept takes, so a Malay speaker should listen to them:

- "pelega" (heard as "pelegar" or "peleger") and "berdehit" (heard as "berdehid"), asthma lines 1 and 2.
- "spacer" in asthma lines 1 and 3. Both should sound the same. For line 3 the voice was given "speiser", because three takes of "Basuh spacer" all came back as "Basus pasar".
- "cirit-birit", gastroenteritis line 2 (heard as "ciri-birit"; best of two takes).
- "anti-refluks", abdominal pain line 2 (heard as "antireflaks"; best of three takes).
- "dekstrosa" (heard as "dextrose") and "hipo", hypoglycaemia.
- "gua sha", dengue line 8 (heard as one word, "guasha").
- The spelled acronyms, which the voice reads with Malay letter names: E. C. G. (stop 1), C. C. A. and A. C. S. (stop 4), C. T. and E. P. (stop 5), N. S. A. I. D. (dengue), M. D. I. (asthma), O. R. S. (gastroenteritis). EMACC is spelled E. M. A. C. C. and EMWATCH is read as "E. M. Watch".
- "discaj" (heard as "discharge") and "triaj" (heard as "triage"), journey stops 1 and 5.
- Head injury line 1 was recorded as three takes, split inside the list after "jika berlaku:" and after "tangan atau kaki,". Listen to the two joins.
