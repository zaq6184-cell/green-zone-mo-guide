# Cancer pain at home: spec

GOAL: a patient going home with cancer pain, or the family member looking after them, hears and sees every line of the patient information leaflet issued with the CPG Management of Cancer Pain, 2nd edition (2023), once, in English (leaflet 1) or Bahasa Melayu (leaflet 2). The MO plays it from the leaflet tab of the cancer pain CPG entry while the patient waits. The sibling of `video/dengue-home`, built the same way.

OUTPUT: HyperFrames 0.9.22, 1080 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC. Delivered as `video/cancer-pain-home-1080.mp4` and `video/cancer-pain-home-ms-1080.mp4`, and 720 × 720 copies for the app (`app/media/cancer-pain-home-720.mp4`, `app/media/cancer-pain-home-ms-720.mp4`) with covers (`-poster.webp`, 720 px) and thumbnails (`-thumb.webp`, 144 px) from the title frame (2.9 s).

TIME: the voice is the clock. The narration is the app's read-aloud take of the leaflet (VoiceStudio: "Google Kira - Female - BM + EN" at speed 0.86 in English, "Malay Kore - Female - TTS Studio" at 0.95 in Malay), recorded one leaflet line at a time, so the start of every line is known to the hundredth of a second (`work/timing-<lang>.json`, written by `work/prep.cjs` from `narration/scripts/read-map.json`, the same marks as `READ` in the app). The film opens 2.2 s before the voice and holds 0.9 s after the last word, then an end card for 14 s (the dengue film's was 7.8 s; this source line is 57 words, and `hyperframes check` asks for about 13 s to read it). English: 253.5 s; Malay: 252.3 s.

WORDS: the leaflet as the app shows it (`P['cancer-pain-cpg'].leaflet`, `src/parts/data7.js`), word for word, in the language of the film: the title, the seven headings (their capitals as printed), the three paragraphs and every item. Nothing on screen says anything the leaflet does not, except the source label, the chip naming the CPG ("Cancer Pain CPG, 2nd edition · Leaflet 1"; "CPG Sakit Kanser, edisi ke-2 · Risalah 2") and the guide's name. The end card carries the source and acknowledgement exactly as the app shows them (`L.src`), in English in both films, as in the app. Two kinds of layout only, the words unchanged: the question that opens an item ("How is cancer pain treated?") is set in the section's jewel, and a list the app joins with semicolons breaks after each semicolon. `work/check-words.cjs` checks every block on screen against `work/leaflet.json`.

LAYOUT: a paragraph that is a sentence ("There are several tools…") is read as a line of its own; a paragraph that opens a list ("Family & caregivers can:", "Contact the following…") stays at the top of the card as its lead. "How can my family or caregivers help?" is a list: each line is added and lit as it is read, and the whole list holds. Every other line comes alone, as in the dengue film. Text is sized by measuring it in Chromium with the film's fonts (`work/build.cjs`, Playwright from `src/node_modules`): each heading, line, list and the source line takes the largest size that fits its box (headings 60 px down to 42 px; lines 70 px down to 40 px; the list 46 px; the source 36 px; nothing under 30 px), and a line with a web address takes a size at which the address fits on one line, so it is never broken.

LOOK:
- Ground: the cancer pain CPG banner loop (`paincpg`, a lavender glass awareness ribbon on dark blue, Flow Studio, already in the app). Its own ends do not meet, so `work/loop7.mp4` is a 7-second loop with a 1 s crossfade at the join (as the app's copy is), cropped square around the ribbon (`assets/bg.mp4`), pushed in from 100% to 110% over the film, under a dark scrim that deepens while the cards are up.
- Cards: white, radius 44 px, on the app's jewel colours by section: plum for What causes and for the family, indigo for assessing, teal for the medicines, emerald for the two reassuring answers, sapphire for where to get help.
- Type: Figtree 700, 800 and 900 (in `assets/fonts`).
- Pictures: stroke icons on a 24 grid, as in the app (the ribbon, pressure, a syringe, a gauge, a 0-10 scale, a face, a heart, a pill, a list, a shield, a calendar, people, a chart, a clock, a hand, a hospital, a globe and a book).

MOTION: as in the dengue film: cards deal in from 40 px below with 1.5° of turn (`power3.out`, 0.45 s). Each line arrives 0.2 s before its first word and leaves 0.5 s before the next. A dot per line marks the place in the section.

SOUND: the voice at -16 LUFS; the Flow Music bed under it at 0.14 volume, fading in over 1.2 s and out over the last 3.5 s. The bed is the Flow Music take "Gentle Reassurance (Extended)" (song 9f9efea2-daf5-4d3d-900b-8afe5a28c2ac, 148 s, made on 23 Nov 2026 with the `flowmusic` MCP: calm piano and warm string pads, 70 bpm, no vocals, no drums; the first take, "Gentle Reassurance" 6d79d3ae, was 53 s, so the Producer was asked to extend it). It is longer than the film with one 6 s crossfade (the take from 0 to 134 s, then from 14 s to its end, so its own fade falls after the film ends), levelled to the dengue bed's -15.7 LUFS so 0.14 volume sits the same (`assets/music.mp3`). One take for both films.

BANNED: any wording not in the leaflet; a face or a voice of a real person; a patient name or number; text that moves while it is read; glow, particles, glass cards.

QA: `hyperframes check` clean in both projects; a frame at every line in both languages, the full family list and the end card; `node work/check-words.cjs` and `node work/check-words.cjs ms`; the rendered loudness.
