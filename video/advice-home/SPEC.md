# Discharge advice films: spec

GOAL: a patient going home, or the family member looking after them, hears and sees every line of the guide's discharge advice for their condition once, word for word, while the MO finishes the discharge. One short film for each of the seven advice tabs the app reads aloud: mild traumatic brain injury, dengue fever (the ebook's pathway), the Asthma Zone, acute gastroenteritis, renal colic, abdominal pain and hypoglycaemia. Made on 24 Nov 2026 at Krishna's "all five". The siblings of `video/dengue-home` and `video/cancer-pain-home`, built the same way.

OUTPUT: HyperFrames 0.9.22, 1080 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC. Each film is its own project, `video/advice-home/<key>/` (English) or `<key>-ms/` (Malay), written in full by `node work/build.cjs <key> [ms]`, and renders on its own. Delivered as `video/adv-<key>-1080.mp4` and, for the app, `app/media/adv-<key>-720.mp4` with `adv-<key>-poster.webp` (720 px) and `adv-<key>-thumb.webp` (144 px) from the title frame. Keys: `tbi`, `dengue`, `asthma`, `gastro`, `renal`, `abdo`, `hypo`.

TIME: the voice is the clock. The narration is the app's own read-aloud take of the tab (`READ['adv-<key>-en']`; VoiceStudio, "Google Kira - Female - BM + EN" at 0.86), recorded one line at a time, so every line's start is known to the hundredth of a second. `work/prep.cjs` joins the section takes as the app's MP3 was joined, checks them against the app's marks and levels the voice to -16 LUFS. The film opens on a title card (no voice) for 4.0 s, holds 0.9 s after the last word, then an end card for 9 s.

WORDS: the tab's lines as the app shows them (`advice` in `src/parts/data*.js`, read in the app's build order), word for word. Nothing else is on screen except the entry's title, "Discharge advice", the source as the app names it (`metaOf`: "Ebook p25–26", or for the Asthma Zone the entry's own source, "Asthma memo, Nov 2025"), on the end card the patient page's source sentence for an ebook tab ("From *The Clinical Pathway in Emergency for Medical Officer*, Emergency & Trauma Department, Hospital Kuala Lumpur (2025).", as the app prints it under the advice on a patient's phone), the label "Source" and the guide's name. Two kinds of layout only, the words unchanged: an opening "Do:", "Don't" or "Avoid" is set in bold in the card's colour, and a line joined with semicolons breaks after each semicolon. `work/check-words.cjs <key> [ms]` checks every block on screen.

CARDS: outcome green (`--discharge`, #0d6f40) for what to do; garnet for a line that tells the patient to come back, return, come to hospital, go to the nearest Emergency Department or seek help (one rule for every film, `BACK` in `work/build.cjs`). A garnet line always has a card of its own, washed in garnet at the top. A line over 100 characters has a card of its own; a run of shorter green lines is gathered into one card as a list that builds up as it is read, each row lit while it is read (up to five rows, as long as they stay at 44 px or more), so a short line ("Get plenty of rest", "Do: tepid sponging") is not gone before it can be read. A list's last row that would leave less than 2.6 s after it is read moves to start the next card instead, and a list that this makes smaller than 44 px passes its own last row on in turn (gastroenteritis 1–3 / 4–5; dengue 1–3 / 4–6 / 7–9). A dot per line marks the place in the tab. One picture per line (`PICS`); a struck-through picture only where the line itself opens with "Don't" or "Avoid", so a picture never says more than its line ("Antibiotics are not required" has a plain mark). Line by line:

- **tbi** (Mild traumatic brain injury, Ebook p25–26, loop `brain`; cards 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+5+6+7 / 8): 2 garnet ("Go to the nearest Emergency Department for: …"), the rest green.
- **dengue** (Dengue fever, Ebook p31–32, loop `infect`; cards 1+2+3 / 4+5+6 / 7+8+9): all green (the tab has no line about coming back).
- **asthma** (Asthma Zone flow, Asthma memo, Nov 2025, loop `flow`; cards 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5): 2 and 3 garnet ("come to hospital if not better", "come straight to hospital"), the rest green.
- **gastro** (Acute gastroenteritis (adult), Ebook p17–18, loop `abdo`; cards 1+2+3 / 4+5 / 6): 6 garnet ("Seek medical help …"), the rest green.
- **renal** (Renal colic, Ebook p53–54, loop `abdo`; cards 1 / 2+3 / 4 / 5+6): 4 garnet ("Seek help at the nearest hospital …"), the rest green.
- **abdo** (Abdominal pain, Ebook p10–11, loop `abdo`; cards 1 / 2+3 / 4): 1 garnet ("Return to hospital immediately …") and 4 garnet ("Return for follow-up at EMACC or EMWATCH in 24 to 48 hours": a planned follow-up, garnet only because the rule reads "return"; Krishna may prefer green), 2 and 3 green.
- **hypo** (Hypoglycaemia, Ebook p43–44, loop `meta`; cards 1 / 2 / 3+4 / 5): 5 garnet ("Seek medical help …"), the rest green.

LOOK: the ground is the entry's banner loop as the app shows it (the entry's `hero`, else its topic group's loop: `brain`, `infect`, `flow`, `abdo` for gastroenteritis, renal colic and abdominal pain, `meta`), each made a 7 s loop with a 1 s crossfade at the join and cropped square around its subject (`assets/bg-<loop>.mp4`), pushed in from 100% to 110% under a dark scrim. A header over every card carries "Discharge advice" and the entry's title. Type, cards, motion and the end card as in the dengue film: Figtree 700, 800 and 900; white cards of radius 44 px; cards deal in from 40 px below with 1.5° of turn; each line arrives 0.2 s before its first word. Text is sized by measuring it in Chromium with the film's fonts (Playwright from `src/node_modules`), nothing under 30 px.

SOUND: the voice at -16 LUFS (each film levelled as a whole to -16 LUFS, two-pass and linear, in `work/finish.sh`: single-pass `loudnorm` is dynamic and lifted the bed under the voiceless title card from about -33 to -15 LUFS); the cancer pain film's Flow Music bed ("Gentle Reassurance (Extended)", `assets/music.mp3`, levelled to -15.7 LUFS) at 0.14 volume, fading in over 1.2 s and out over the last 3.5 s, so the series sounds as one.

MALAY (not made yet): `node work/prep.cjs advice` reads `ADVICE_MS` from `src/parts/data31.js` (one Malay line per English line); `node work/prep.cjs voice ms` needs the takes in `narration/read-aloud/adv-<key>-ms/` and their line timings (`READ_MS` in `data31.js`, or `narration/read-aloud/READ-ms.json`) and stops, naming what is missing, until they exist; then `node work/build.cjs <key> ms`, `node work/check-words.cjs <key> ms` and `sh work/finish.sh <key> ms`. A Malay film shows "Nasihat penjagaan di rumah" (the patient page's Malay heading) for "Discharge advice", the app's Malay source sentence (`ADV_MS_PT`), and the entry's English title, since the app has no Malay titles for these entries: for Krishna to decide. Colours and pictures follow the English line of the same number. Make the Malay films only once Krishna has checked the translation.

BANNED: any wording not in the tab; a face or a voice of a real person; a patient name or number; text that moves while it is read; glow, particles, glass cards.

TO REMAKE (in `video/advice-home`): `node work/prep.cjs` (the advice, the voice, the grounds and the bed); then for each key `node work/build.cjs <key>`, `node work/check-words.cjs <key>`, `cd <key> && npx --yes hyperframes@0.9.22 check`, and `sh work/finish.sh <key>` (renders, levels the sound to -16 LUFS in two passes, `level` to skip the render, writes the 1080 film, the 720 app copy at CRF 27 with AAC 96k, and the cover and thumbnail). One render at a time. Never run `publish`.

TITLE CARD: the entry's title, "Discharge advice", the source as the app names it, and the guide's name (as the brief asks; the name is also on the end card).

CHECKED (24 Nov 2026), on the final builds and renders: `check-words.cjs` passes for all seven (every line word for word, each once, only the allowed extras); `hyperframes check` passes with no errors or warnings for all seven (14 to 30 contrast checks each). Frames at the title card, every line, each list when full and the end card were read for each film (`work/snap-<key>-en/`), and the final 1080 files were read back at the title card, abdominal pain's list and dengue lines 8 and 9 (`work/final-frames/montage.jpg`). The advice, titles, sources and app words were re-extracted from `src/parts` after the renders and are unchanged. Lengths and loudness of the 1080 films: tbi 71.0 s, dengue 51.6 s, asthma 61.4 s, gastro 50.6 s, renal 55.0 s, abdo 40.1 s, hypo 52.1 s; each -16.0 LUFS (abdo -15.7), peaks -2.7 to -4.0 dBFS; the bed alone under the title card -34.5 to -36.0 LUFS, the voice -15.4 to -16.7 LUFS. The 720 copies: -15.8 to -16.0 LUFS, peaks -1.5 to -3.1 dBFS. Not heard by ear; not seen on a phone.
