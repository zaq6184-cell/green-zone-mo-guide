# What's new in the guide: spec

GOAL: a new MO or HO, or anyone who has not opened the guide for a while, sees in about a minute what changed in the Green Zone MO Guide that matters on shift, on the guide's own screens. Played from the What's new page. Features only: no dose, criterion or clinical statement (standing rule 8), no patient detail (rule 4), nobody's name.

OUTPUT: HyperFrames 0.9.22 (pinned: `npx --yes hyperframes@0.9.22`; never `publish`), composed at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC. Delivered as `video/whatsnew-1080.mp4` and, for the app, `app/media/whatsnew-720.mp4` (1280 × 720, libx264 CRF 25, AAC 96k, `+faststart`, as the explainer's copy), with a cover `app/media/whatsnew-poster.webp` (1280 × 720) and a thumbnail `app/media/whatsnew-thumb.webp` (256 × 144), both from the closing frame at 58.5 s (the title, "Open it any time." and the guide's name, with no caption on it), as the explainer's cover is its closing frame.

SCRIPT (the narration, word for word; 12 sentences, 134 words, one VoiceStudio take each):

| Beat | # | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Open | s01 | What's new in the Green Zone guide? |
| | s02 | Here is what changed for you on shift. |
| 2 Sooner, and pick up | s03 | On a weak signal, the guide now answers sooner. |
| | s04 | And the home page offers the last step you opened, so you can pick up where you left off. |
| 3 Films and the intro | s05 | The dengue and cancer pain leaflets now have short films, in English and Bahasa Melayu. |
| | s06 | The first-shift intro can be read aloud in either language. |
| 4 Antidotes and abbreviations | s07 | In Shift tools, the antidotes quick guide is a tap away. |
| | s08 | Type an abbreviation in search to see what it stands for. |
| 5 X-rays and calculators | s09 | Reading X-rays shows real films with the normal anatomy outlined. |
| | s10 | Tap a part to find it on the film. |
| | s11 | And over fifty calculators each name the source they are built from. |
| 6 Close | s12 | Open What's new any time to see what changed since you last looked. |

Each line is a feature of the built guide, from the What's new rounds (`V8` in `src/parts/engine.js`): the page in two parts and the pick-up card (23 Nov 2026), the leaflet films (dengue, 9 Nov; cancer pain, 23 Nov), the intro read aloud (23 Nov), the antidotes and abbreviations (21 Nov), Reading X-rays with its outlined films (11 and 12 Nov), the calculators from their own sources (16 Nov; 56 in the hub), and Changed since you last looked (18 Nov).

VOICE: VoiceStudio, "Google Kira - Female - BM + EN" (e4e6e5d8), language en, speed 0.86, no `instruct`; one take per sentence (`assets/vo/s01.wav` … `s12.wav`), each transcribed back (whisper.cpp large-v3-turbo) and checked against the script. The voice is the clock: word times from whisper set every caption and every move.

MUSIC: one Flow Music take made for this film with the `flowmusic` MCP on 24 Nov 2026, "Clinical Updates Background Bed" (song 3a061937-d384-44fe-886f-9ebd660737fa; soft upright piano, light wooden marimba, warm synth pads, soft shaker, subtle brushed kick, 100 bpm, no vocals; 70.4 s). Levelled to -15.7 LUFS (as the leaflet films' beds) into `assets/music.mp3`, played under the voice at 0.16 volume, fading in over 1.0 s and out over the last 3.5 s. The voice is levelled to -16 LUFS; the final mix is levelled to -16 LUFS integrated, true peak -1.5 dBTP or lower.
