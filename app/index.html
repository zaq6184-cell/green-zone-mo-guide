        What's new in the guide

        Version 8 is a new design with the zone map, whole-pathway maps, visual tools and a first-shift intro. Below it, what came in from the MO Drive and the triage course folder, what did not and why, and what is still to confirm.

        The wall poster

        Two A3 sheets for the Green Zone wall: the patient flow, rules and numbers on the first, and the pathways, EMACC, Asthma Zone and referrals on the second. Tap a sheet to read it full size. With night colours on, you see the night versions; the sheets on the wall keep the day colours.

        Sources and about

        Everything in this guide and where it comes from. It is a reference tool only and holds no patient data. Use clinical judgement and consult the Emergency Physician on duty when unsure.

        Privacy

        No patient data is entered or stored. Saved and recent pathways, the quiz score, your settings, the intro and where you stopped the tour stay in this browser only, and you can clear them in Settings. Nothing you tick in the safety check or type in the calculators is kept.

        Illustrations and clips are generated artwork and show no real staff or patients. Department documents are cited by title and date; personal phone numbers, registration numbers and patient details in them are left out.

        Reference tool only. Pathways follow The Clinical Pathway in Emergency for Medical Officer, Emergency & Trauma Department, Hospital Kuala Lumpur (2025), as written, and page numbers refer to that edition. Green Zone rules and department protocols follow the memos, SOPs and notes in the department's drives, with each source and date named on its entry.

        Use clinical judgement and consult the Emergency Physician on duty when unsure. Version 8, 8 Nov 2026.
