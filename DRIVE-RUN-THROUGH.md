# MO Drive run-through for the Green Zone guide

Updated for version 7 (7 November 2026): the six triage course folder documents added in version 7 are listed in their own section below.

Folder: **3. CLINICAL** in the ETD HKL MO Drive (https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1o3QbTFpn9zbQz9d6gWjIUz633cU9jWRP), read on 7 November 2026.

The folder holds 31 subfolders and 2 PDFs at the top level. I listed every subfolder and read each document that bears on Green Zone work. This page records, folder by folder, what went into the guide and what did not, and why.

## How the Drive material sits in the app

- The ebook, *The Clinical Pathway in Emergency for Medical Officer* (HKL ED, 2025), stays the approved source. Its 23 pathways are built as written.
- Department documents from the Drive are added as clearly labelled notes. On a pathway they appear in a panel headed **From the MO Drive: department notes**, each with its source and date. New topics carry a **New** badge, and the Asthma Zone flow carries **Updated** because the November 2025 memo replaced the earlier flow.
- The **What's new** page in the app (`#new`) shows this same list.

## What I deliberately left out

- The sample slip in the 2022 Green Zone memo shows a real patient's name and IC number. Neither is copied.
- The MERS PUI flowchart gives a named specialist's personal mobile number. It is not copied; the app says to go through the GIM specialist, as the flowchart does.
- The MAU admission flow chart lists consultants' registration numbers. They are not copied.
- The drug regimens in the MOH child abuse guideline (2009) are out of date. They are not shown; the app says to check them with ID or the paediatric team.
